Implement deterministic recording and replay for Jenkins Pipeline in the workflow-cps plugin.

Add a block step named deterministicReplay with mode set to record or verify and a required tapePath. Reject invalid paths, unknown modes, or nested blocks with errors containing tapePath must be a relative path, Unknown deterministicReplay mode, or cannot be nested.

In record mode, execute the body and save the steps and outcomes for later verification. In verify mode, compare the body with the recorded tape and replay recorded step outcomes without repeating side effects. Block steps such as node, dir, or withEnv are checked against the tape, but their bodies still run and the steps inside them are replayed. Treat extra or missing steps, steps in a different order, and mismatched step names or arguments as drift. On drift, stop the build either by interrupting it with a Result.ABORTED cause (for example org.jenkinsci.plugins.workflow.steps.FlowInterruptedException) or by failing with hudson.AbortException; any other exception is not an acceptable way to report drift. The drifting step and any steps after it must not run, and a try/catch in the pipeline body must not be able to catch the drift and let the build continue.

A tape is stored under the build's replay-tapes directory. If no earlier recorded tape is available, the build fails with a message containing recorded execution tape <tapePath>.
